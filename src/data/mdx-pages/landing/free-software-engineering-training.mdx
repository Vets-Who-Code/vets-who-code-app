## What free means here

Free means $0. There is no tuition, no income share agreement, no deferred payment plan and no hidden fee. You do not sign over a percentage of your first paycheck and you do not take on a loan.

The program is paid for by donors and partners. Vets Who Code is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 86-2122804, and what we teach is published in full so anyone can check it.

## What you get in 17 weeks

- **A structured curriculum.** The Hashflag Stack runs in four phases: Foundations, Software Engineering, AI Engineering and Production Mastery. Every skill on it is validated against labor-market demand. [Read the curriculum](/curriculum).
- **A mentor who is a working software engineer.** Your mentor operates like an engineering manager: 1-on-1s, code review, hard questions and help with career decisions.
- **A body of work.** Blogs, pull requests, shipped features and verified work. It is the evidence a hiring manager can read before they ever speak to you.
- **Sessions with people on the calendar.** Remote-first is not self-serve. The platform carries the material that does not change so the human hours go to pairing and building.

## Who is eligible

U.S. veterans, active-duty service members and military spouses. The program is fully remote, so you can train from anywhere in the country. No prior experience with software is required; we select on aptitude, drive and follow-through.

## Free training is not a bootcamp

Bootcamps sell seats. Vets Who Code is a nonprofit software engineering accelerator, and the difference shows up in how it runs:

- **Cost.** $0, funded by donors, instead of tuition or an income share.
- **Cohort size.** 10 to 15 troops, selected, instead of as many seats as will sell.
- **Mentorship.** An engineering manager model, instead of a teaching assistant.
- **Outcome.** A software engineer with a verified body of work, instead of a certificate.

Our incentive is your outcome, not a tuition check.

## How to apply

1. Complete the [prework](https://github.com/Vets-Who-Code/Prework). It is public, and it is how you show us follow-through.
2. [Apply](/apply). The application takes about 12 minutes.
3. Interview. We schedule interviews as the cohort acceptance date approaches.
4. Join the cohort.
