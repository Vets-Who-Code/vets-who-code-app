## No degree required

Admission is selective, but we evaluate aptitude, drive and follow-through, not credentials or prior technical experience. No prior experience with software is required. If you can show up to sessions, do the reps between them and take feedback, you have what we screen for.

## No tuition, and who pays instead

The program costs $0. There is no tuition, no income share agreement, no loan and no hidden fee. Donors and partners fund it. Vets Who Code is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 86-2122804.

## What replaces the degree

Employers hire on evidence. Instead of a transcript, you leave with a body of work:

- **Blogs** that explain your decisions in writing.
- **Pull requests** opened, reviewed, revised and merged, with the conversation still attached.
- **Features** shipped to real users in real codebases.
- **Verified work**, where every skill names the artifact that proves it and the criterion it is judged against.

The curriculum behind it is public: every skill, every link between skills and the reason each one is there. [Read the curriculum](/curriculum) before you apply.

## Who this is for

- You served, and you are ready to work like it: showing up to sessions and doing the reps between them.
- You want to build software, not collect certificates.
- You can hold a job, a family or a recovery alongside 20–30 focused hours a week.
- You would rather be told the standard than be told you are doing great.

## Who this is not for

- You want a credential you can finish by watching videos.
- You want someone to place you in a job rather than help you earn one.
- You want to work entirely alone. The sessions are the program, not an add-on.

## How to start

1. Complete the [prework](https://github.com/Vets-Who-Code/Prework).
2. [Apply](/apply). It takes about 12 minutes.
3. Interview. We schedule interviews as the cohort acceptance date approaches.
4. Join the cohort. Seventeen weeks, remote-first, $0.
