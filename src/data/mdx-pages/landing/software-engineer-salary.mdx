## The starting number

Vets Who Code graduates enter the field in software engineering roles, and the program's target for average starting salary is **$72K–$85K**. That is a target the program is built to hit, not a guarantee to any individual. Your offer depends on the role, the company, the market you are in and the body of work you bring to the interview.

## The industry band for the role

The role is software developer or software engineer, Standard Occupational Classification 15-1252. In the curated labor-market data behind our [career guides](/career-guides), the average salary for that role sits in a **$105K–$115K** band. It is an average across all experience levels, so treat it as where the role goes, not where it starts.

## What moves the number

- **The stack and its depth.** The [curriculum](/curriculum) runs through Foundations, Software Engineering, AI Engineering and Production Mastery. Production AI engineering is in demand, and it moves the number.
- **Seniority.** The industry band above includes engineers years into the role. Pay climbs with each year of shipped work.
- **The body of work.** Blogs, merged pull requests and shipped features are what let you negotiate from evidence instead of hope.
- **Location and company.** Remote roles at national employers pay differently from local ones.

## What it costs to get there

$0. There is no tuition and no income share agreement, so the first paycheck is yours. The accelerator is 17 weeks, remote-first, and funded by donors and partners.

## Where graduates work

Microsoft, Accenture Federal Services, Amazon, Google, GitHub, Booz Allen, Deloitte and other companies across the industry. 97% of graduates land software engineering roles, and alumni earnings total more than $20M collectively.
